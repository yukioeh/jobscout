## Job Summary
This is an Executive Director (ED/604) Product Management and AI Transformation role within CIB Marketing, accountable for the strategy, roadmap, and delivery of the CIB marketing technology portfolio and for leading the multi-year AI transformation agenda for CIB Marketing.

The role leads a product organization with product managers/owners aligned to specific platforms and spanning multiple tools, and operates through close partnership with CIB Marketing teams, Channel and Business Owners (including automation team, web team, Marketing Ops, and CMO teams), Technology, Controls, JPMC Marketing, and enterprise partners. The role is expected to think and act strategically, drive results, innovate, communicate clearly (especially in uncertainty), and uphold a strong risk and controls environment.

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. Product & Portfolio Leadership (Martech + AI)
- Lead the CIB Marketing Product organization (4–6 direct reports), including product owners dedicated to specific platforms and product owners spanning multiple tools; establish clear goals, coaching, and consistent product delivery standards.
- Own the multi-year product strategy and roadmap for the marketing technology portfolio, balancing near-term delivery needs with longer-term platform modernization and interoperability.
- Accountable for the marketing technology budget and financial discipline across the portfolio, including business cases, prioritization trade-offs, and ROI/value tracking; coordinate closely with Technology partners where software expense is held outside Marketing.

### 2. AI Enablement, Adoption, and Change Management
- Own the multi-year AI transformation roadmap for CIB Marketing, defining priorities, sequencing, milestones, adoption targets, and impact metrics; drive disciplined execution against approved outcomes. Partner with Technology peer marketing organizations, and platform teams to deliver end-to-end capability outcomes, including roadmap direction, value realization, and integration execution across systems.
- Establish the measurement architecture for AI initiatives using an economic levers approach, ensuring focus on a prioritized set of initiatives.
- Build AI enablement and communications so marketers understand what capabilities are available, what’s coming, where to start, and how to access expertise and development resources; reinforce responsible usage patterns and scalable adoption.
- Own the marketer-facing AI capability playbook, including a catalog of approved capabilities, patterns, and “where to start” guidance that keeps pace with rapid change.

### 3. Intake, Prioritization, Governance, and Executive Transparency
- Drive intake, prioritization, and resource planning to enable agile deployment: translate business priorities into sequenced work, manage dependencies, and drive trade-offs transparently.
- Chair and run an integrated governance cadence covering both marketing technology portfolio governance and AI transformation/use-case governance, providing decision-ready materials and clear transparency on progress, risks, and outcomes.
- Provide central governance of the AI use case pipeline by enabling decentralized experimentation while holding decision rights to scale or sunset pilots based on measured impact.

## What Success Looks Like (12–18 months)
Success is expected to be outcome-based and measurable:
- **Martech roadmap and platform health:** Key roadmap commitments delivered with improved platform reliability and a clear modernization path.
- **AI leadership and enablement:** An adopted, marketer-facing AI playbook with scaled use cases tied to measurable outcomes (productivity, performance, revenue, strategic intelligence).
- **Transparency and governance:** A single, disciplined governance cadence with decision-ready materials, clear trade-offs, and consistent executive reporting.
- **Product org execution:** A high-performing product team delivering faster throughput, benefit-based roadmaps, clear alignment of priorities, and a sustainable controls-by-design posture.
